# v1.17.13 Atlassian overlays, Japanese keycap previews
**Protocol stays at 23.** This app and firmware 1.9.1 can be updated independently. ja-US needs firmware 1.8.9 or later, and the input-method key needs 1.8.5 or later. With older firmware, neither appears.

## 1.17.10 – 1.17.13: More app overlays 🧩
- **Jira, Bitbucket and Confluence overlays now match each app's own `?` shortcut dialog**, with Fluent icons. Earlier keys that were wrong are gone.
- **Confluence whiteboards get their own overlay**, with the drawing tools on S, T, E, L, H and V.
- **GNOME Files (Nautilus) has an overlay.**
- **Logged-in GitHub tabs get the GitHub overlay without the browser extension**, recognised by the window title.

## 1.17.9: The forwarder comes back after an update 🔁
- **On GNOME, the forwarder's tray icon reappears after a self-update.** Before, the app restarted without an icon.
- **Only one forwarder runs per user.** A second start exits.

## 1.17.4 – 1.17.7: Japanese and Korean keycaps 🗾
- **ja-US, Japanese on a US keyboard layout, sorts under Asia with Japan's flag.**
- **The layout editor can preview any language's keycaps** and draws the new input-method key (한/영, 英数/かな).
- **The flags font bundle now holds one flag per country (v6).** The app re-flashes it once on the next connect.

## 1.17.2, 1.17.3 and 1.17.8: Dialog fixes
The firmware flash and update dialogs show their status in your language. Resetting the keymap now warns that it deletes your macros too. The firmware update bar no longer falls behind the transfer, and the log bundle offers timeframes from 5 minutes to 3 days.
