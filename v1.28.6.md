# v1.28.6 Brightness in Settings, and a colour for every section
The protocol stays at 36. Host 1.28.6 works with firmware 1.13.9, and you can update the host and firmware separately.

## 1.28.6: Integrations in cards
- **Each Integrations page shows its content in a white card** (black in the dark theme) on the coloured section, like Settings and Tutorials.

## 1.28.5: A colour for every section 🌈
- **The sections of Settings, Integrations and Tutorials run through the rainbow**, red to pink, in the light and the dark theme.
- **Controls match their section:** an on/off switch, a ticked checkbox and the main button (Install, Copy Extension, Start) take the section's colour.
- **The note at the top of a section** ("Some settings could not be read…") is easier to read on the coloured background.

## 1.28.4: VS Code extension dependency
- The VS Code extension's build uses glob 14.0.6, which closes a security advisory in one of its dependencies (minimatch).

## 1.28.3: Brightness moves to Settings 💡
- **Settings → Brightness → Keycap brightness** has the brightness pictures from the old tray submenu: Automatic, Off, 1%, 50% and 100%. Pick one and click OK. The keyboard does not report its brightness, so no picture is selected when the dialog opens.
- **Automatic** follows the daylight again when Follow daylight is on. When it is off, the keyboard returns to its own stored brightness.
- **The tray menu has 12 rows**: the Brightness submenu is gone.
- **Integrations and Tutorials have the coloured sections of Settings:** a colour stripe in the list on the left and the same colour behind the page.
- **The Settings list on the left moves to the next section earlier while you scroll**, when its title is in the upper third of the window.
