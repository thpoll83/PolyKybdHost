# v1.21.14 Tray tiles and rounded controls
The protocol moves to 30 for Auto Shift. This app works with keyboard firmware 1.11.5, but its Auto Shift settings stay greyed out until firmware with protocol 30 is published. Update this app before the firmware.

## 1.21.14: Rounded controls ✨
- **Buttons, text fields and lists have rounded corners**, in the light and the dark theme.
- **Buttons next to each other share one width** in every dialog.
- **Dialogs keep their proportions on a scaled display** (125 %, 150 %): their sizes grow with the text.

## 1.21.13: Tray menus as tiles 🧩
- **Brightness, Idle Display, Keycap Size and Keycap Script show a keycap preview for each choice.** Updates, Maintenance and Help & About show large icons.
- ⚠️ Tiles show on Windows, and on Linux desktops where the app draws its own tray menu. On KDE, on GNOME with AppIndicator and on macOS the menus keep their text list.
- **Dialog buttons and icons scale with the display**, so at 150 % they no longer look smaller than before.

## 1.21.12: One look for messages 💬
- **Every confirmation and error message shows the app's own icons** on the box and on every button, instead of the system's old warning and question symbols.

## 1.21.11: Auto Shift ⇧
- **Tap-Hold Timing… has Auto Shift**: hold a key a little longer to type its shifted character, with a hold time of 100 to 500 ms. On the command line: `polyctl tap-terms --auto-shift on --auto-shift-ms 180`.
- ⚠️ Needs keyboard firmware with protocol 30. With older firmware the Auto Shift controls are greyed out.

## 1.21.10: Refreshed dialogs 🎨
- **One icon set on every dialog button.** The crash, problem and error alerts size their details box to the text.
- **The update prompt opens at a comfortable reading width**, as tall as its notes.
- **The log viewer's colours are readable on the light theme.**

## 1.21.9: Copilot overlay
The Copilot app (`copilotapp.exe`) now gets its overlay too.

## 1.21.8: Settings dialog
The settings cards stretch with the window instead of stopping at a fixed width.
