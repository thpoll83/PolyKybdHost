# v1.17.40 Faster app switching, new overlays

**Protocol stays at 23.** This app and firmware 1.9.9 can be updated independently.

## 1.17.39: Faster app switching ⚡
- **The keycaps follow a new window after 500 ms instead of 1 s.** The overlays now arrive 0.5–0.75 s after a switch; before, they took 1.25–1.5 s.
- Windows you pass for less than 500 ms, such as during Alt+Tab, still get no overlays.

## 1.17.35 – 1.17.38: New overlays 🎨
- **Microsoft:** OneNote (about 150 shortcuts; OneNote windows had no overlay before), Visio, the Copilot app and copilot.microsoft.com, and Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote on the web. Paint and Photos get ESC marks.
- **KiCad 10:** new sets for the PCB Editor, Schematic Editor, Footprint Editor, Symbol Editor, 3D Viewer and GerbView, read from KiCad 10.0.7's default hotkeys. They replace the old 14-icon PCB overlay.
- **Mathematica and Wolfram:** 83 keys on Windows and Linux, 67 on macOS, for Mathematica up to 14.0 and Wolfram 14.1 and later.
- **Music:** FL Studio, Ableton Live 12, REAPER, Audacity 3 and 4, and MuseScore Studio.

## 1.17.40: Your location stays out of the logs 🔒
- **A failed daylight-brightness lookup no longer writes your coordinates to the log**, which the problem report attaches. Before, the error line held the latitude and longitude to about 11 m.

## 1.17.33 – 1.17.34: Faster first switch, Windows setup
The app loads its image libraries in the background at start, so the first overlay switch does not wait for them. The log splits each switch's time into device calls and host work. On Windows, setup now installs comtypes, which the shortcut reading uses.
