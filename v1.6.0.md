# v1.6.0 Keycap icons from the keyboard's flash

**Protocol 18 → 20.** Update this app first, then the keyboard to firmware **1.2.0**. With older firmware everything keeps working, but every keycap image is still uploaded in full.

## 1.6.0 and 1.5.0 — Much faster app switches ⚡
With firmware 1.2.0, a cold switch to an app with many shortcuts went from 208 reports and 16.7 s to 32 reports in under a second.

- **Shared shortcut icons now live on the keyboard.** The app flashes a 56 KB library of 599 icons on the next connect, then sends an icon number instead of the image (cmd 42).
- **Other small images are compressed and share a report** (cmd 41).

## 1.5.3 – 1.5.8 — Every app shows its own ESC mark 🎨
- **Apps that share a template now each show their own logo on ESC**, including every Chromium browser. Before, ESC showed the template's logo or stayed blank.

## 1.4.0 — IDE shortcuts per OS
One IDE overlay now follows the vendor's keymaps for Windows, macOS and Linux and adds about 65 shortcuts. Before, macOS showed Ctrl where the IDE uses Cmd.

## 1.3.7 – 1.5.5 — Windows fixes
The tray no longer dies at startup when another program holds `settings.yaml` open. A crash dump no longer takes the background service down. The taskbar no longer shows File Explorer's shortcuts.

Plus maintenance releases 1.3.5, 1.3.6, 1.5.1, 1.5.2 and 1.5.4 🧹
