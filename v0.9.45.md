# v0.9.45 — Snappier overlays & sharper legends

> Protocol unchanged at **v11** — pairs with firmware 0.9.74.

## 0.9.45 — Snappier overlays ⚡
Switching between apps and windows no longer redraws keycaps that aren't changing.
- The host now drops a redundant overlay enable/disable instead of paying a full
  72-keycap refresh + split-sync for no visible change — a browser or terminal
  animating its title bar no longer thrashes the displays every poll. Genuine
  app/site switches redraw exactly as before.

## 0.9.43 — Sharper keycap legends 🔤
Reshipped font-pack bundles render key legends grid-fitted, so symbols and several
scripts come out crisper on the OLEDs.
- The `symbol`, `mideast`, `syllabic`, `asia` and `fantasy` bundles auto-flash on the
  next connect — no manual step.
- The font-pack inspector/preview (power users, `--debug`) now honours the same
  hinting, so its preview matches the hardware.

*Plus tooling & maintenance in 0.9.44. 🛠️*
