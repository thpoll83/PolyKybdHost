# v1.21.4 Tap-hold timing

The protocol moves from 28 to 29. Update this app first, then the firmware to 1.11.3. On older firmware the app still works, but Tap-Hold Timing is greyed out.

## 1.21.4: Tap-hold timing ⏱️
- **Tap-Hold Timing… in the tray menu** sets the tapping term and the Flow Tap term, with Restore Defaults. From the command line, use `polyctl tap-terms [--tapping MS] [--flow-tap MS]`.
- ⚠️ Needs firmware 1.11.3. On a split42, the Flow Tap field is disabled.

## 1.21.3: Translated font-pack notice
The notice that font packs are out of date while Auto Flash is off is now translated in all 19 catalogs.
