# v1.21.17 Icon library v8
The protocol stays at 30. This app works with keyboard firmware 1.11.5 and 1.11.6. Auto Shift needs firmware 1.11.6.

## 1.21.16: Icon library v8 🖼️
- **29 new icons**, and the icons most apps use now have the lowest numbers, so an app switch sends fewer reports.
- ⚠️ This version renumbers the icons once. On the first connect after the update, the app sends the new library (about 350 KB) to both halves. Until then it sends those icons as images. With **Update on connect** off, run `polyctl fontpack sync`.

## 1.21.15: Bundles checked again after a link drop 🔁
- **The app checks the font and icon bundles again when the keyboard reports a change**, not only at connect. A half that missed a bundle now gets it within seconds, instead of at the next reconnect.

## 1.21.17: Smaller menu tiles
The tray menu tiles are smaller, and their captions wrap onto at most two lines.
