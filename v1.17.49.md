# v1.17.49 Old Rev.2 boards, meeting overlays

**Protocol moves to 24.** Update this app first, then the keyboard to firmware 1.9.12. With older firmware the app still connects; only the Rev.2 setting is unavailable.

## 1.17.49: Old Rev.2 boards (2023) 🔧
- **The two halves of an old Rev.2 board connect again.** Maintenance → Fix Left/Right Side has two new entries that set or remove the Rev.2 mark, one half at a time. `polyctl handedness <side> --split-vbus on|off` does the same. Needs firmware 1.9.12.
- Only boards marked "Rev 2 04/2023" (left) and "Rev.2 03/2023" (right) need it. The steps are on [Old Rev.2 Boards](https://www.polykybd.org/reference/old-rev2-boards/).

## 1.17.42 – 1.17.48: New overlays 🎨
- **Meetings:** Teams and Zoom are rebuilt from their official shortcut pages, and Teams gets a separate set for the browser and Linux. New: Google Meet, Cisco Webex, Webex Meetings and Microsoft 365 Copilot.
- **Safari:** the web-app overlays (GitHub, Notion, Meet, Teams on the web and others) now load in Safari too.
- **Google and Adobe:** Sheets, Slides, Gmail, Calendar, Lightroom Classic, InDesign and Acrobat.
- **CAD, science and tools:** FreeCAD, OpenSCAD, Autodesk Fusion, Thunderbird, Jupyter, RStudio, MATLAB, Overleaf, SumatraPDF, Okular, VLC, Spotify, Affinity, Postman, DBeaver, GitHub Desktop, draw.io, Excalidraw and Linear.
