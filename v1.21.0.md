# v1.21.0 Emoji search by keyword

The protocol moves from 27 to 28. Update this app first, then the firmware to 1.11.0. On older firmware the app still works, but emoji search is greyed out and the font-pack wait for Eden is off.

## 1.21.0: Emoji search by keyword 🔍
- **Emoji search dictionaries in 79 languages.** The app writes the chosen one to the keyboard; English is the default. Choose under *Selected Language → Emoji Search Language*, in Settings, or with `polyctl emoji-dict`.
- **Your own dictionary.** Edit a dictionary as a text file, build it with `emoji_dict.py build <id> --user`, and pick it with *Own dictionary file...* or `polyctl emoji-dict file`.
- **Tray menus no longer wait on the background service.** Menu state loads and clicks are sent in the background, so a stalled service cannot freeze the tray.
- ⚠️ Search needs firmware 1.11.0 on a split72.

## 1.20.11 – 1.20.18: New overlays 🎨
- PTC Mathcad Prime (Windows), SideFX Houdini, QGIS, Autodesk Maya and Avid Pro Tools.
- The Space key shows a hold-Space hint in Paint.NET and Houdini.

## 1.20.13 – 1.20.15: Firmware signature download
The signature download is retried for about a minute when it fails, and the progress dialog shows each attempt.

## 1.20.10: No font flash over Eden 🌿
- **With firmware 1.11.0, the app waits for the Eden intro or screensaver to end before it writes font packs on connect.** Before, one half could stay in Eden after a firmware update.

## 1.20.9: Icon and font-pack fixes 🔧
- **Overlays with icons numbered above 2047 are sent again.** OpenRA – Dune 2000 showed icons on only 3 or 4 keys.
- **With Auto Flash off, the app now says when font packs are out of date**: in the log, once per session as a tray message, and in the Updates menu.
- OpenRA game art is also found in the Flatpak's folder.

## 1.20.5 – 1.20.7: GDB script and emulator icons
The GDB script is installed only when you tick it, and is written to the init file GDB reads on macOS and with `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`. RetroArch and MAME icons keep 2 px from the key edge.
