# v0.11.0 — Firmware signing, end to end 🔐

**Update this before your next firmware update.** Keyboards from v0.11.0 ask you to
confirm an unsigned firmware image on the keys, and a host that doesn't know about that
answer simply stalls part-way through the flash.

### Firmware signing
- Flashing an unsigned build now shows *"Confirm on the KEYBOARD…"* and waits while you
  press the highlighted **A** (accept) or **R** (reject) key. Cancelling the flash takes
  the prompt back off the keycaps.
- Choosing a `.bin` with no `.sig` beside it says so immediately, instead of letting you
  find out at the end of a 446 KB transfer.
- A refused flash now says *which* reason: no signature, a `.sig` that doesn't match its
  `.bin` (two files from different builds, or a damaged one), or a genuine transfer error.
  All three used to be reported as "CRC mismatch".
- There is deliberately no way to accept a signature that fails to verify. Only a
  *missing* signature can be confirmed, and only by a physical keypress on the keyboard —
  a confirmation clicked in this app would travel the same USB channel that signing exists
  to protect.

### Also
- `polyctl fw version` asks the keyboard instead of answering from a cache, so it is
  usable as evidence right after an update. It reports the image size and CRC32 too.
