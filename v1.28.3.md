# v1.28.3 Brightness in Settings
The protocol stays at 36. Host 1.28.3 works with firmware 1.13.9, and you can update the host and firmware separately.

## 1.28.3: Brightness moves to Settings 💡
- **Settings → Brightness → Keycap brightness** has the brightness pictures from the old tray submenu: Automatic, Off, 1%, 50% and 100%. Pick one and click OK. The keyboard does not report its brightness, so no picture is selected when the dialog opens.
- **Automatic** follows the daylight again when Follow daylight is on. When it is off, the keyboard returns to its own stored brightness.
- **The tray menu has 12 rows**: the Brightness submenu is gone.
- **Integrations and Tutorials have the coloured sections of Settings:** a colour stripe in the list on the left and the same colour behind the page.
- **The Settings list on the left moves to the next section earlier while you scroll**, when its title is in the upper third of the window.
